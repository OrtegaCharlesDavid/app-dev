# My Favorite Movie/Series is the Alice In Borderland
Alice in Borderland is a Japanese show based on a comic by Haro Aso. It’s about a guy named Arisu and his friends who suddenly end up in an empty Tokyo. They are forced to join dangerous games to stay alive. Each game is harder depending on the playing card shown. The story shows how people change, work together, and fight to survive. It became popular because of its exciting action and emotional story.
